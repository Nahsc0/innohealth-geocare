# Kaduna sandbox user guide

## Find healthcare

Open **Find healthcare**. Search by state, facility type, name or LGA. For nearby results, use your GPS with permission, pick a point on the map, or enter both coordinates and choose **Use these coordinates**. Postcode validation shows its source and asks you to supply a location whenever coordinates are unavailable. The map loads only when opened. You can search and open facility details without it.

Open a facility to check verification date, services, operating hours and phone, where available. With road routing configured, select an origin to calculate a driving estimate. For Hausa, select **Hausa** in the header.

## Emergency assistance and tracking

Call **112** in Nigeria for a real emergency. GeoCare runs sandbox simulations only. To practise, enter a fictional category and location, confirm the information is synthetic and submit. Save the displayed reference and private tracking key. In **Track request**, enter both values to see the simulation status.

## Partner workflows

Sign in with an administrator-issued sandbox account. Use in-app navigation to retain the session. A full reload requires signing in again.

- A dispatcher can move a submission to awaiting acknowledgement, acknowledge it, assign an available sandbox unit or flag it for supervisor review. Assigned responders can record en route, arrived and completed. Dispatcher and responder can leave sandbox messages on the assigned incident.
- A clinician can select a verified receiving organisation facility, enter a synthetic case reference and reason, and record the applicable basis for the exercise. The receiving organisation acknowledges, confirms handover and completes the simulation. The sender can cancel a pending referral.
- An administrator can verify organisations, create and deactivate partner accounts, draft or publish facility records, enter sourced community centroids, review audit events and set detail-retention days.
- An analyst can review grouped counts, download CSV and compare verified settlement centroids with their nearest mapped primary healthcare centre. Treat these results as estimates for the entered data only.
